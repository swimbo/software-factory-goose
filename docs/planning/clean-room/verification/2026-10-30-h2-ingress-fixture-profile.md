After new registration and the owned Restate restart, compare completed old invocation identity and exact bounded result across snapshots, preserve old nonterminal work, and accept new work only on the verified new deployment. Unknown response formats and missing rows fail closed.

Native keeper testing and the actual fixture sequence are separate proofs. A fresh profile does not establish production admission, candidate evaluation, promotion, or any effect on existing shared services.
