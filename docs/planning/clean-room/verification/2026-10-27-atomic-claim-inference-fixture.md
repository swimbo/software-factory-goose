# Same-object claim and inference admission experiment

`AtomicInferenceFixture` is an unregistered fresh Restate namespace. A single exclusive object stores a fixed fixture task/worker lease, token and attempt, a 750,000-microUSD integer budget, and at most 256 immutable call bindings. Prepare and begin verify the same stored lease and a journaled local clock sample under the exclusive object lock. The binding includes request and review digests, model, amount and lease identity. Revocation is irreversible; reinitializing with the same lease does not clear it. Retained state is validated on each operation, and no record is evicted or reservation refunded.

The ordering contract is explicit: **revocation first** denies begin; **begin first** records `DispatchingUnknown`, retains the reservation, and later revocation blocks further begins but cannot retract that already admitted external action. A lost begin acknowledgement cannot be retried as a fresh begin. No provider is called by this object. The clock is sampled inside the handler; this experiment does not prove the lease remains live until a subsequent external network send.

Three test-first focused tests and strict all-feature/all-target control-plane Clippy passed. The rebuilt disposable Restate/PostgreSQL replay passed twice. It exercised both deterministic orderings, a simultaneous begin/revoke race, forged lease token and changed request conflicts, budget exhaustion, lost begin acknowledgement, and exact reinitialization after revocation. Revoked and unknown outcomes survived graceful and SIGKILL Restate restarts. The SIGKILL followed completed journal operations; it did not interrupt an in-flight mutation. The separate peer-catalog fixture still reproduces its own cross-object late-revocation race; it is not wired to this experimental authority.

This fresh namespace cannot safely operate alongside existing `ClaimLeaseJournal`, `BudgetCounter` or `InferenceAuthority` as another authority for the same unit. Lease initialization, call data and revocation credentials are fixture-supplied and not tied to an authenticated real worker or operator. No trusted catalog producer, lease renewal/reassignment, old-state migration, daily budget, provider response or credential broker is implemented. A trusted launcher must still make at most one provider call after begin, and no mechanism here cancels or prevents a previously admitted call from being sent after revocation. Production routes and live services remain disabled.
