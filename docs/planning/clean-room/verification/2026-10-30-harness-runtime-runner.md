Reviewed enforceable charge bounds for all hidden calls and retries are also absent.

The recorded governance metadata fingerprint is explicitly partial, not a full route attestation. No route, provider key, pricing table, deployed service, production ledger or factory fence was changed. Production transition/launch evidence, all-path bounds and complete provider-billed receipts must be established before the first paid call. Then the same frozen suite must actually complete 120 attempts within the preserved caps and pass all safety, acceptance, cost and time thresholds. Until then Phase 12 remains blocked and the baseline remains selected.
