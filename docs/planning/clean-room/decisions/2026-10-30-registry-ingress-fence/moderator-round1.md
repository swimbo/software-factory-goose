# Moderator synthesis and round 1

All three researched positions prefer one disposable test of pinned Restate
Unix-only administration plus retirement of every ungated SDK. None selects a
live policy before that proof. Network filtering remains a fallback if the Unix
configuration or permission boundary fails; do not build both alternatives first.

The shared conclusions are: the lock/count-only proposal is insufficient;
SDK1–4 must all be covered; 8080 forwarding can bypass a direct-port-only fence;
old pinned work must be classified rather than called drained when unavailable;
unknown registration cannot be repeated; and Root, PID1, the daemon and UID2004
journal role remain trusted.

Please resolve these exact implementation details in the final round:

1. Is a positive same-UID 8080-to-old-SDK effect, followed by verified permanent
   old endpoint retirement and failed direct/forwarded effect attempts, sufficient
   for the Unix candidate? Do not require installing hypothetical nft rules just
   to demonstrate a bypass that the selected design does not use.
2. Restate creates admin.sock under its private UID2004 data tree. Root with no
   capabilities may also lack traversal there. Prefer retaining strict data/socket
   permissions and executing fixed Root-owned admin operations as trusted UID2004
   through PID1, with Root-owned scripts, no caller-supplied target/SQL/body/FD,
   bounded pipe responses and Root receipts. This is Root-mediated administration
   and trusts UID2004, not a claim of a UID0 server connection. Is that acceptable
   without new groups, privileged firewall authority or relaxed data permissions?
3. Can the experiment use a new factory-owned Restate namespace and data root,
   two synthetic SDKs and disposable control/worker probes, testing actual Unix
   path/modes/ACLs, no TCP admin, same-UID direct and 8080 forwarding before/after
   retirement, old pinned/queued classification, one registration/lost response,
   and owned restart? Only exact owned resources may be cleaned up. Host reboot,
   shared namespace loss and live retirement remain later gates, not destructive
   operations in the spike.
4. For later live cutover, require a persistent closed Root intent; preserve a
   recoverable journal backup and all deployment histories; stop/socket-fence all
   ungated endpoints before the decisive count; install a restart-safe refusal
   against old endpoint reactivation; and admit only SDK5 after exact attestation,
   registration receipt and all-history replay. Any nonterminal old invocation
   blocks normal completion unless explicitly retained as unavailable quarantine.
   No silent cancellation, history deletion, force registration or reopen.

Reply with agreement or a concrete unresolved objection. This round selects the
bounded experiment and conditional direction; actual production acceptance still
requires its measured result and a review of the exact live transition plan.
