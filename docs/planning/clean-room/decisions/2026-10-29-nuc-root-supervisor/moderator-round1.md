# Moderator round 1

All three papers support a narrow root-owned, systemd-supervised fixed-operation authority outside Docker, root-protected opaque task catalog and one-way admission closure, dedicated task cgroup, and independently available authenticated stop. The observed canary is adequate to implement this boundary, not to activate general production admission. Reject Docker-only and unsupervised custom-root alternatives for availability/lifecycle; keep native clone3 as an unselected fallback.

## Resolutions proposed for one concession round

1. **Operator scope:** use existing bo UID1000 as human operator, distinct from new nonroot control/worker/evaluator/journal and brake-frontend identities. No existing account/group/SSH/sudo changes. The already user-resolved O4 record expressly says no resistance-to-same-UID-compromise or MFA claim (`2026-10-26-operator-auth/consensus-plan.md`, post-debate selection). Thus do not impose a new human SSH key or an impossible claim against bo's existing Docker-root power. Root/Docker administrators remain trusted.
2. **Brake:** independently supervised nonroot brake frontend with a dedicated service identity, separate stop bearer/custody and root backend permission restricted to stop-only fixed targets. Ordinary operator authorization cannot be used as brake credential. Two secrets readable by bo are not MFA or bo-account-compromise resistance; document that limit. A stolen approval bearer without kernel operator identity or stop key must have zero brake authority. Test availability without control/database/gateway/Docker. Distinct stronger human brake-custodian protection is not silently invented as an MVP blocker where O4 excludes same-UID-compromise claims.
3. **Lifecycle:** root helper must own/protect per-generation catalog, one-use intents and permanent closure. Freeze+kill+same-inode empty can be reported separately from reconciled engine closure. Non-force exact-ID removal is conditional on pinned engine/daemon and Moby source locking; unknown effects remain frozen/closed. Do not stop/thaw an unknown generation through a manager job. Owner-loss/restart, held/lost start and cgroup-migration denial are pre-admission tests, not waived by the canary.
4. **Deployment:** identities and root-owned state may be installed with no service activation after exact PLAN review and collision/preservation controls. Private SDK/journal networking and root-owned socket activation require actual positive/negative canaries before private runtime admission. The first limited catalog may preauthorize one exact greenfield recipe; it is not general multi-task decomposition or completed INT-LOOP. No budget waiver beyond this experiment.
5. **Recovery acceptance:** real shared daemon/systemd/host disruption is not authorized. Implement fail-closed startup/quarantine and test a task-owned isolated equivalent or simulations with their exact limits. Do not claim actual host power-loss proof from fsync or service SIGKILL. Retain a specific open gate if the required recovery property cannot be tested safely; do not manufacture a passing overall grade.

Each reviewer: maintain/concede with primary evidence, explicitly distinguish implementation permission from production acceptance, and state any remaining HIGH disagreement.
