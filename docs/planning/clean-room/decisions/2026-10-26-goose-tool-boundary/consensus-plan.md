# Consensus — bounded Goose boundary spike

2026-10-27. Three reviewers, primary-source research and one challenge round. Agreement is limited to a **falsification-first offline spike** with the exact digest-verified v1.49.0 binary and a recording local model endpoint. Evidence is adequate for that experiment and insufficient for production activation or final architecture selection.

Test fresh restricted configuration first, including project plugin/hook discovery, forbidden/unadvertised calls, resumed sessions, subagents and all enabled tool paths. Require positive controls. Reject a candidate on bypass, secret leakage, false completion, unaccounted cost or uncertain process cleanup. Verify source-side masking across errors, notifications, structured output, resources and split secrets; unsupported forms fail closed. Separately resolve the scope of R13 descendant command enforcement; OS effect isolation alone is not proof every descendant command was policy checked.

Hooks-only and a PATH shell shim are rejected as sufficient evidence. A newer release or a minimal pinned patch may be evaluated only against reproduced gaps and maintenance/replay tests; no automatic upgrade, patch or library rewrite was selected. Error/cancellation and unknown spend/side effects cannot be upgraded to success by later model text or exit zero. Lease reassignment requires verified cleanup or quarantine.

Reviewers agree on the experiment; they do not claim the boundary exists. Keep live worker and privileged services disabled pending canary evidence and separately verified identities, gateway budgets, durable claims and exact-revision acceptance. Sources, positions and round responses are in this directory.
