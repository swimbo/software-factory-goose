## Current evidence boundaries

- Local component evidence is stronger than the 2026-10-26 register: absent-component wording has been corrected, but no gap is closed or severity waived here.

Synthetic prices and provider responses do not establish real Bifrost billing, Goose tool traffic, authenticated ingress or ambiguous-ack reconciliation. [Gateway integration](verification/2026-10-27-gateway-real-restate-fixture.md).
- A separate unregistered idempotent reservation object passed real disposable Restate lost-ack, concurrency and restart tests. It cannot be adopted alongside an existing ledger: trusted per-call IDs, exclusive migration and authenticated scope are still missing. [Reservation experiment](verification/2026-10-27-idempotent-reservation-fixture.md). A separate bounded-output live-leader fixture is not integrated or production containment. [Capture experiment](verification/2026-10-27-live-leader-capture-spike.md).
- An unregistered inference authority combines fresh-namespace reservations with request-bound one-way dispatch fencing and passed disposable Restate lost-ack/concurrency/restart tests. No provider was called; a trusted sole launcher, authenticated lease binding, private ingress and exclusive old-ledger migration remain required. [Inference authority](verification/2026-10-27-inference-authority-fixture.md).
- A feature-gated inherited-group execution fixture prevents the tested nested shell escape under an owned live leader, but its parent check is not authentication and it does not prove setsid escape, owner-loss recovery or safe output release. A synthetic gate blocked direct test-socket egress and duplicate/forged requests, but lacks peer authentication and production task admission. [Containment fixture](verification/2026-10-27-live-leader-execution-boundary.md), [gate fixture](verification/2026-10-27-inference-gate-egress-fixture.md).
- The execution shell supervisor currently ignores process-group signal errors; a fail-closed prototype exposed macOS `EPERM` for already-exited leaders and broke ordinary commands. This remains an open containment blocker, not a passing cleanup proof. [Investigation](verification/2026-10-27-execution-group-signal-failure.md).
- The queue now rejects newly scheduled transport-ambiguous identifiers; existing stored identifiers may require migration. A disposable restart latch and surviving-guardian controller-loss fixture improve crash response evidence, but do not establish escaped-process containment or guardian-loss teardown. The Rust Goose request and reservation experiments remain unregistered synthetic fixtures, not the real gateway or durable ledger. [Queue](verification/2026-10-27-queue-key-alignment.md), [latch](verification/2026-10-27-watchdog-recovery-latch.md), [guardian](verification/2026-10-27-watchdog-controller-loss.md), [Rust reservation](verification/2026-10-27-goose-rust-reservation-fixture.md).
- Native Restate restart and claim SQL-loss recovery are real disposable-service checks. They do not prove full factory recovery, private ingress, old-handler migration or recovery after loss of both journal and SQL.
- Patched Goose results use fixed local providers and protected fixture configuration. They do not prove production billing, mandatory launcher use, complete tool coverage or the final runtime architecture.
- Holdout transport and authenticated adapter positive fixtures do not establish separately provisioned accounts. Watchdog stand-ins and cooperative cancellation are not a production independent-stop proof.
- Promotion's branch fence has no automatic clear/reset. Further fresh mutation is deliberately unavailable until a verified recovery design exists; a matching Git SHA alone does not establish attribution or prove that an old action has stopped.
- Later enterprise, delivery, complete Phase 13 artifact/story audit, zero-gap and final sign-off gates remain pending. Historical local test counts are evidence snapshots, not acceptance grades.
